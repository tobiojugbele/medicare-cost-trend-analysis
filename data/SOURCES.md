# Data Sources

## 1. CMS Medicare Geographic Variation Public Use File (GV PUF)
* **Dataset Title:** Medicare Geographic Variation - by National, State & County
* **Source:** Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (data.cms.gov)
* **Download Date:** October 2026
* **Format:** CSV (Downloaded as ZIP)
* **Data Coverage:** Multiple years (Verify the specific years once unzipped)
* **Description:** Contains geographic breakdown data for Original Medicare (Fee-For-Service) beneficiaries regarding per-capita spending, service utilization, and risk scores.
